# Apply to the group

The group works on real-time 3D vision and immersive media — view synthesis,
plenoptic imaging, and neural rendering. I supervise PhD students and
postdoctoral researchers at the LISA laboratory (ULB). This page explains how
to apply for a **PhD**, a **postdoc**, or an **internship**. Please read it
fully before writing to me.

## PhD

I may not have funding available for a PhD position. The realistic route for
most candidates is a **grant-funded PhD**: you identify a fund (FRIA, PDR,
ERC, or a comparable programme) and write the proposal, with my involvement.

I will get involved and help with the application **only if the idea
convinces me and you are a strong candidate**. Grant applications are hard
and time-consuming to write, so I cannot take on candidates who come to me
with the goal of having a grant application written. Come with a clear,
well-thought idea that fits the group's research directions
([research areas](/about/)) and with evidence that you can carry it out.

## Postdoc

Postdoc positions are project-based, and I may not have funding available.
As with PhDs, a **grant-funded postdoc** is the realistic route: the same
conditions apply — the idea must fit the group and convince me, and I can
help with the application under the same constraints as for PhDs.

## Internship

Internships in Belgium **are not paid**. This is the default: unless there is
a **convention between your institution and ULB**, the internship is
unpaid. If such a convention exists for you, it applies, and the internship
is organised under it.

## How to apply

Write to me at [{{ site.email }}]({{ 'mailto:' | append: site.email }}) with:

1. **Subject line (mandatory):**
   `[APPLICATION] [POSITION] [FUND] Student name`
   - PhD with a specific fund: `[APPLICATION] PhD FRIA Jane Doe`
   - PhD without a fund identified yet: `[APPLICATION] PhD NONE Jane Doe`
   - Postdoc: `[APPLICATION] Postdoc ERC John Smith`
   - Internship: `[APPLICATION] Internship CONVENTION Marco Rossi`
     (or `NONE` if there is no convention)

   **Emails whose subject does not follow this format are ignored**, without
   reply.

2. **Body:** the main idea of your proposed project — a few short paragraphs
   are enough — and your **CV** (attached). Add more information only if you
   consider it necessary.

3. **What to expect:** I receive several applications, and I need to discuss
   each one with my team — including the long-term strategy of the group —
   before deciding. **It may therefore take some time to get an answer.** I
   only respond to applications that pass the first screening; no reply does
   not mean the idea was bad, only that it is not a fit right now.
